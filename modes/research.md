# Research
Investigate a decision or question with a bounded set of useful primary sources. Establish publication and access dates; label foundational work honestly. Extract mechanisms, supporting and contrary evidence, assumptions and limits. Trace claims to sources and distinguish your synthesis from what each author demonstrated.

Compare ideas across sources when useful. Connect findings to the current question and propose a bounded experiment with a prediction and stopping condition when transfer is uncertain. Research need not force an implementation.

Deliver a concise synthesis, source links/dates, implications, unknowns and a next decision or experiment. Done means the question is answered to the available evidence or its precise uncertainty is exposed. A twice-weekly reading loop is configured only when explicitly requested; verify timezone, destination and actual delivery separately. Never imply automatic wake, universal access or guaranteed continuation.
