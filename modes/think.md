# Think
Clarify the decision, constraints and observations. Distinguish causes from correlations and assumptions from known facts. Develop materially different plausible explanations or options only where they change the decision. Preserve strong existing paths while challenging their weakest premises.

For consequential alternatives, state supporting and contrary evidence, causal predictions and what would falsify the premise. Name important unknowns and choose the smallest useful test or next decision. Do not stretch an incoherent idea merely to keep it alive.

Deliver a reasoned recommendation, trade-offs, uncertainty and the next actionable decision. Done means the user can choose or test a path from explicit evidence; unresolved questions remain unresolved rather than becoming confident conclusions.
